                Projects — Build in Public

                Share what you're building, what you need, and find collaborators — while learning how to protect your ideas and join teams the right way.

                🛡️ Build Smart. Protect the Vision.

                Before joining a project or sharing sensitive details, innovators should understand expectations, ownership, credit, and confidentiality.

                1. Define the idea

                Write a simple summary of the problem, solution, audience, and current stage.

                2. Clarify ownership

                Decide who owns the original idea, who contributes, and how future credit will work.

                3. Use agreements when needed

                For serious projects, consider NDAs, founder agreements, contributor agreements, or written collaboration terms.

                4. Track contributions

                Keep notes on who designed, coded, researched, funded, promoted, or developed key parts.

                5. Protect before pitching

                Avoid revealing secret sauce publicly until you understand patents, copyrights, trademarks, or trade secrets.

                6. Join teams intentionally

                Know your role, time commitment, expectations, and what happens if someone leaves the project.
